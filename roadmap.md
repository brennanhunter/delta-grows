# Delta Grows Website Roadmap

## 1. Purpose and audience
- Define the core goal clearly: educate the Mississippi Delta community, attract supporters/partners, and make program registration simple.
- Keep the audience front and center: local farmers, aspiring agricultural entrepreneurs, funders, donors, and community partners.
- Use a primary message like “Empowering Delta farmers with sustainable agriculture, business training, and generational wealth” to anchor the homepage.

## 2. Structure and navigation
- Keep top navigation simple and sticky: About, Courses, Gallery, Resources, Contact.
- Add a clear homepage hero that explains what Delta Grows does, who it serves, and the main visitor action (learn more, join a course, support the mission).
- Ensure every page has a clear path back to the homepage and a visible Contact/Support link.
- Use descriptive section headings, not generic labels. For example: “Our Mission,” “Training Programs,” “Impact Stories,” “Ways to Get Involved.”

## 3. Content strategy
- Lead with mission and impact: use the mission from `public/relevant_docs/impact_docs/Delta Grows Initiative YR 2.docx`.
- Make key facts scannable: impact metrics, number of farmers trained, partnerships, program outcomes.
- Organize programs like course pages into clear blocks: course title, description, schedule, cost, and next steps.
- Use storytelling: describe the problem in the Mississippi Delta, the solution Delta Grows offers, and the difference it makes.
- Keep paragraphs short and use bold text for key phrases (as shown in your About Us page screenshot).

## 4. Visual design and branding
- Maintain the current clean, white space-focused aesthetic, but improve visual hierarchy with stronger contrast between headings and body text.
- Use large headings and bold section titles like your current pages, but add consistent spacing and supporting subheadings.
- Use imagery of people, farms, and training activities. Your current hero and About Us image style is strong; expand with more real project photos.
- Avoid too much empty header space on pages. Keep the logo and navigation compact so the content appears sooner.
- Support brand color accents with an accessible palette, especially for buttons and links.

## 5. Accessibility and usability
- Ensure text has adequate contrast against backgrounds; dark body text on white is good, but check colored sections for readability.
- Add `alt` text to all images and videos, describing subjects and purpose.
- Make clickable links and buttons large enough on mobile. Your desktop nav looks good; confirm the mobile menu is usable.
- Use meaningful link text: “See course details” instead of “click here.”
- Structure pages with headings (`h1`, `h2`, `h3`) so screen readers can follow the flow.

## 6. Trust and credibility
- Highlight your partnerships and funding support using the `2025 DELTA GROWS Partnerships Matrix 2.docx` and the About Us copy.
- Add a visible “Supported by the Walton Family Foundation”-style trust line on the homepage and About page.
- Create an impact section that includes measurable outcomes, local stories, and partner logos.
- Link directly to key resources that demonstrate transparency: financials, initiative docs, and handouts.

## 7. Program pages and resources
- Make the Courses section a hub, not just a gallery. Each course should have a dedicated summary card.
- For the Intro to Agriculture course page, keep the content structure shown in your screenshot: description, safety focus, logistics, cost.
- Add a registration or inquiry CTA on every course page, even if the class is free or TBD.
- Use the Resource page to surface documents and videos from `public/relevant_docs/youtube_videos.md` and `public/relevant_docs/impact_docs/`.
- Consider adding a Resources subpage for downloadable PDFs and videos with short descriptions.

## 8. Homepage improvements
- Add a concise value proposition in the hero section that says who you help and how, referencing “sustainable agriculture, business training, and economic opportunity.”
- Add a quick “How Delta Grows works” section with three steps: Learn, Train, Grow.
- Add a “Featured impact” row with numbers and short stories, based on your initiative and partnership documents.
- Add a “Latest videos and resources” preview to link directly to your video content.

## 9. SEO and discoverability
- Use descriptive page titles, meta descriptions, and alt text focused on keywords like “Mississippi Delta agriculture training,” “sustainable farming education,” and “Delta Grows nonprofit.”
- Keep page URLs meaningful: `/about-us`, `/courses/intro-to-ag`, `/resources`.
- Use structured content for course details so search engines can understand your programs.

## 10. Recommended next steps
1. Create a stronger homepage hero and add a clear primary CTA.
2. Expand the About page with impact metrics and partner highlights from `Delta Grows Initiative YR 2.docx`.
3. Improve the Courses hub so each program has a card, outcome summary, and sign-up/inquiry action.
4. Build a Resources page that surfaces videos, handouts, and initiative documents.
5. Audit mobile navigation, text contrast, and image alt text for accessibility.
